Welcome to Aquarian Age Thailand Fan-site アクエリアンエイジ タイ ファンサイト

A fan-made companion for the trading card game Aquarian Age. Everything is in one place: the full Japanese card list with English names and text, the official rules in Japanese and English, a deck builder, and a game table where you can play your decks, alone to test them or online with a friend.

Unofficial fan site. Card images and rules © BROCCOLI. Nothing to install or sign up for: your decks and settings are saved in this browser.

How to use

  1. Learn the rules firstRead the Rulebook in Japanese, English or both.
  2. Find cardsSearch and filter the Card List.
  3. Build a deckAdd cards in the Deck Builder; it is saved in a deck slot.
  4. PlayOpen the Game Table, choose your deck and play.

Card List

Every card, in Japanese and English.

  • Search by Japanese or English name, or by card number (e.g. A3-01 0035).
  • Filter by series, faction, card type, class icon, skill and stats; sort by stats.
  • Click a card for the large image and its text in both languages.

Rulebook

A Japanese–English guide to the official rulebook (Ver1.1).

  • Switch between 日本語, Both and English.
  • Use the contents list to jump to a section.
  • For the exact wording, check the official rulebook.

Deck Builder

Build and save your decks.

  • Tap + on a card to add it, and + again for more copies (up to 4 of a card); − takes one out.
  • Drag a card to reorder it or to move it to another column.
  • Several deck slots; Import / Export as text or an image; Templates to get started; choose a card sleeve.

Game Table

Play with the decks from your deck slots.

  • Solo: choose Deck 1 and Deck 2 and play both sides, to test a deck.
  • Online: create a room and share its code; the other player joins with their own deck.
  • Card rules run by themselves where they are built; the rest is marked ✋ “do by hand” with tools for it. You can Undo your last action.
